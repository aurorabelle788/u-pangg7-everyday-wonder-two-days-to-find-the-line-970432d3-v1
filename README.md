A single sentence about a small everyday moment of wonder: a lift mirror
catching its rider mid-yawn, and the half-second when your own face looks
borrowed from a stranger.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the sentence to
`/output/entry.txt`. No network, no secrets, deterministic output.
